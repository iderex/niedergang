# NNNN. Title

## Date

YYYY-MM-DD, the day the decision was taken rather than the day the file was
written, where the two differ.

## Status

One of: proposed, accepted, superseded by NNNN, withdrawn. A record is never
edited into a different decision.

## Question

What had to be decided, in a form somebody who was not there can restate. If
the question changed while it was being answered, this section carries the
question that was actually answered.

## Options considered

Every option that was genuinely on the table, including the ones that were
rejected quickly, with what each one costs. An option nobody weighed does not
belong here; an option weighed and dropped in a sentence does.

## Decision

The option taken, stated once and without hedging.

## Reasons

Why that option was taken.

## Reasons against

Why it might be the wrong one, in the words somebody arguing against it would
use. A record that lists only the winning argument cannot be revisited, because
the reader cannot tell what was weighed.

## What would change this

The measurement, the finding or the outside constraint that would make this
record wrong, written now rather than reconstructed later. Where a decision
rests on a number, this section names the number and the command or the source
that produced it.

## What depends on this

The issues, records and parts of the tree that would have to be revisited if
this record were superseded, kept current as they appear.
